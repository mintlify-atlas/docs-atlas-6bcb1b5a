**9social-user-leo** is a minimal, opinionated starting point for developers who value clarity over clutter. Stripped down to its essentials, it ships with exactly what you need to begin — and nothing you don't. Whether you're prototyping a new idea or studying the anatomy of a purposefully lean project, this repository is designed to get out of your way and let you build.

## The Lion That Squeaked

The tagline *"The lion that squeaked"* captures the project's spirit perfectly. A lion carries enormous potential — strength, presence, ambition — yet here it makes the smallest possible sound. That's intentional. Big goals don't require a loud launch; they require a deliberate one. **9social-user-leo** starts quiet so that everything you add on top of it can roar.

## Design Philosophy

**9social-user-leo** is built around three core principles:

- **Minimalism** — Every file earns its place. The repository contains only what is necessary, making it trivial to understand the entire project in minutes.
- **Zero dependencies** — There are no package managers to run, no lock files to resolve, and no third-party libraries to audit. The project stands entirely on its own.
- **Single-artifact output** — The project produces one meaningful artifact: the `Whoa` file. This constraint is a feature, not a limitation. It forces focus and makes the project's intent immediately legible.
